📖 HOW TO PLAY

🎮 MOVEUse ← / → or A / D. On phones or tablets, hold the arrow buttons. Moving drains hunger, and empty hunger slows you down.
❤️ STAY ALIVEA 🔪 knife or 🗡️ enemy sword removes 1 heart. An 🪓 axe removes 2 hearts. At zero normal hearts, the game ends.
🍎 FOOD & GOLDEN APPLESRegular apples and cute 🥭 mangoes restore hunger. A ✨ golden apple grants 2 temporary golden hearts; they disappear as damage consumes them.
🧪 MYSTERY POTIONAll potions look the same. Each one randomly restores up to 3 normal hearts OR deals 3 hearts of damage.
⚔️ RARE OBLITERATORThe glowing crossed-swords pickup is safe but rare. Collect it to get the Obliterator for 5 swings. Picking up another refreshes its durability.
💥 SLASH & SCOREWith the sword, press RIGHT CTRL or tap SLASH. Nearby axes and enemy swords get chopped. Earn 10 points for each chopped target; the sword breaks after 5 swings.

Every item falls inside the arena. Keep moving, collect the useful drops, and watch out for hazards. Sound can be switched off at any time.
